## Documented deviation

**The stream sits 3 m below the plan's level.** `CITY_LAYOUT.md` §9 asks for stream water at **+2.0 m** and the pool at **+6.0**, fed off the ridge. The level builds them at **−1.0** in a 10 m cut. This is not a slip and it is not fixable by a setout parameter: at the East Gate the plan also puts the **East Approach road at 0 m**, running out to the map edge through the same crossing that the stream passes under. Water at +2.0 would flow two metres *above* the road it is bridged over, and the East Bridge deck (+1.2) would sit below the water it spans. The +2.0 figure only works if the ground east of the wall rises with the foot of the ridge, which is the phase-E terrain item (*banks, fields, ridge*), not a greybox attribute. Until that pass the stream is cut into the flat plane, which is the only self-consistent reading of the plan's own sections.

**Everything else the pictures fix is built to the millimetre** — see the table: gate footprints and clear passages, the river's centreline and 16 m width at both crossings, both bridge spans with 1.2 m decks and three steps per bank, road widths, district extents, the cathedral's 38 m west front / 44 m towers / 58 m flèche, the Ø28 auction drum on its forecourt, and the two ridge terraces under the fall.
