Warm roofs.
Pale stone. One spire.

A grounded, painterly late-medieval city in high autumn. Dense oak-framed frontages below one Gothic landmark; an inhabited floodplain before the gate, a quiet library close, a controlled auction court, and dark back alleys behind every row.

Status: all nine sheets are painterly concept studies in one shared style. Composed views say on the sheet where they bend camera height or depth for readability.

Concept only. No engine or Blender assets changed. Labels are functional, not lore. Docs/CITY_LAYOUT.md governs the setout; V3 is the current map. Production questions are in the art bible.
